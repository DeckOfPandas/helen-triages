source: "Tom Kerridge"
serves: "6"
prep_time: "QQ"
cook_time: "12 mins"
main_ingredients: [peaches, double cream, eggs, amaretto, crème fraîche, plain flour]
star_ingredient: fruit
tags: [bakes, dessert, fruit, showstopper]
ingredient_groups:
  - items:
      - "2 eggs"
      - "4 egg yolks"
      - "100 g caster sugar"
      - "250 g double cream"
      - "50 g plain flour"
      - "50 ml amaretto"
      - "6 small peaches, cut into quarters"
      - "Icing sugar, for dusting"
      - "300 ml crème fraîche"
      - "1 tsp lemon thyme leaves (optional)"
method:
  - "Preheat the oven to 200°C (220°C non-fan). Whisk together the eggs, egg yolks, caster sugar, double cream and flour, then stir in the amaretto."
  - "Place the peach quarters neatly in six ovenproof dishes arranged on a baking tray, then pour in the batter and bake for 12 minutes."
  - "When puffed up and golden brown, remove from the oven and dust with icing sugar. Add a good spoonful of crème fraîche and a sprinkle of lemon thyme leaves if using. Serve while it sits all puffed up and proud."
