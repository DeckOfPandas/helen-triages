method:
  - "Whisk the eggs, egg yolks, caster sugar, double cream and flour together, then stir in the amaretto."
  - "Place the peach quarters neatly in six ovenproof dishes on a baking tray, then pour in the batter and bake at 200°C for 12 mins."
  - "When risen and golden brown, remove from the oven and dust with icing sugar. Add a good spoonful of crème fraîche and a sprinkle of lemon thyme leaves. Serve immediately."
