# Helen Triages Food

I am the user of this site. I built it to solve two problems: 

1. What shall I cook, out of everything I already know how to make?
2. What new recipe shall I try next? And give me a convenient way of accessing it that doesn't require me to a) be able to find a magazine/email/screenshot, or b) scroll through pages of boring nonsense about your favourite frying pan or how great your children are.

This site tells me what to cook. It does not tell me much about how to cook, because I can already cook. So if you want instructions on how to thicken a sauce, or what the Maillard reaction is, then I suggest you look elsewhere.

(No, you can't see the to-do recipes, they only build locally and are committed to a private repository. Three reasons: 1. I don't want to break copyright. 2. I don't want to disrespect authors. 3. I don't want to put in effort rewriting a load of recipes I haven't tested because many will turn out to be terrible.)

A few recipes carry more troubleshooting than seems reasonable. The length of extra writing is proportional to how much that food has personally wronged me.

Filter by what you have in your hand, by what's lurking in your fridge and going off, or by being in the mood for a giant pile of cheese. Honestly I don't care — this site is not for you. 

Now stop reading recipe websites and go and actually cook something.

## FAQ

### I have a great idea/recipe/criticism for your site. Will you build/try/hear it?

No. I welcome pull requests with thanks. But I'm not interested in pleasing you, in the same way you're not interested in this one time I made courgette jam for a picnic, or memories evoked when my house smells of bread.

### The site looks naff on my phone

I cook from my 13-inch iPad.

### Did you write any of this code yourself directly?

Absolutely not. Not one single line, for better for worse, mostly better. I certainly wouldn't have >3000 tests if left to my own devices.

### Can you share the prompts or scripts or workflows you used to ingest and clean your recipe data?

No. I didn't log these things or even really think them through that hard...welcome to the future: it looks Claude-y. I don't have Repeatable Analysis Pipelines, I have Claude Code, a lot of tests, and slightly too much time on my hands at the moment.

### Can you write a blog post about what you learned about AI-assisted development?

Yes I can. I doubt that I will.
