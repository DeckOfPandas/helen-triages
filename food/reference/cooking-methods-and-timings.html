    Other methods:

    • En papillote: most white fish fillets (cod, haddock, sea bass, sea bream, plaice)

    Timing rule of thumb:

    • Pan-fry/grill: ~3–4 min per cm thickness per side
    • Oven-bake at 220–230°C (fan) ~4 min per cm thickness total (~10 min per inch).

    Endpoint is opaque and flaking if not deliberately rare. See internal temperatures for salmon and tuna.
