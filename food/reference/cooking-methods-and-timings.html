Fish and shellfish

Not cooked by time.

Fish

Cod / haddock
Fillet Pan-fry with dredge (flour/breadcrumb); oven-bake; poach; steam
Whole Oven-bake (less common form for these species)
Halibut / turbot
Steak Pan-fry + oven finish; poach
Fillet Pan-fry + oven finish; poach; oven-roast
Whole (turbot) Whole roast
Herring
Whole Pan-fry (often dredged in oatmeal); grill/BBQ; soused/pickled (no heat)
Fillet Pan-fry
John Dory
Whole Roast
Fillet Pan-fry; steam
Mackerel
Whole Grill/BBQ (classic); oven-bake; smoke
Fillet Pan-fry
Monkfish
Fillet/tail Pan-fry + oven finish; roast whole tail; poach
Plaice / sole
Whole Pan-fry, dredged ("meunière" style); grill
Fillet Pan-fry
Red mullet
Whole Pan-fry; grill/BBQ; oven-bake
Fillet Pan-fry
Salmon
Fillet Pan-fry (skin-down first); oven-roast
Whole Whole roast; poach
Steak/cutlet Poach; grill/BBQ; pan-fry
Sea bass
Whole Roast; steam (esp. Chinese-style); grill/BBQ
Fillet Pan-fry, skin-down
Sea bream
Whole Roast; grill/BBQ; salt-baked
Fillet Pan-fry
Skate
Wing Pan-fry; poach; steam
Swordfish
Steak Grill/BBQ; pan-fry + finish; sear
Trout
Whole Pan-fry (classic "amandine" style); oven-bake; grill/BBQ; poach
Fillet Pan-fry
Tuna
Steak Sear (pan or grill), often left rare/blue centre

    Other methods:

    • En papillote: most white fish fillets (cod, haddock, sea bass, sea bream, plaice)
    • Deep-frying (battered): cod, haddock, plaice fillets
    • Ceviche/raw: sea bass, sea bream, tuna

    Timing rule of thumb:

    • Pan-fry/grill: ~3–4 min per cm thickness per side
    • Oven-bake at 220–230°C (fan) ~4 min per cm thickness total (~10 min per inch).

    Endpoint is opaque and flaking if not deliberately rare. See internal temperatures for salmon and tuna.

    Shellfish

    Abalone
    Pan-fry, briefly; braise slowly
    Clams
    Steam; pan-fry/sauté briefly
    Cockles
    Steam; boil briefly
    Crab
    Boil whole, then pick; steam whole
    Crawfish
    Boil (classic, often spiced); steam
    Langoustine (Dublin Bay prawn/scampi)
    Grill/BBQ; poach/boil; pan-fry
    Lobster
    Boil whole; steam whole; split and grill/BBQ; split and oven-roast
    Mussels
    Steam (classic, e.g. white wine); pan-fry/sauté briefly
    Octopus
    Braise/simmer low and slow first, then char on a very hot grill/pan to finish
    Oysters
    Raw/shucked; grill/BBQ briefly; steam
    Prawns/shrimp
    Pan-fry/sauté; grill/BBQ; poach/boil; steam
    Scallops
    Pan-sear (very hot, short); grill/BBQ; poach
    Squid
    Pan-fry/sauté, very hot and fast; deep-fry (classic "calamari"); grill/BBQ; braise slowly
    Winkles
    Boil briefly, then pick out with a pin
