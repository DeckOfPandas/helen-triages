Internal Temperatures

Pull temperatures and endpoints, protein by protein. "Pull at" is the temperature to take something off the heat; the figure after rest is what it actually reaches once carryover cooking finishes the job — the two are not the same number, and going by the resting figure while it's still in the oven will overcook it.

  • Poultry (chicken, turkey, goose, duck)
  • Beef
  • Pork
  • Lamb
  • Ham (fresh and cured)
  • Steak

Poultry

All whole birds go by the thigh, not the breast — the thigh is the last part to come up to temperature.
