Internal Temperatures

Pull temperatures and endpoints, protein by protein. "Pull at" is the temperature to take something off the heat; the figure after rest is what it actually reaches once carryover cooking finishes the job — the two are not the same number, and going by the resting figure while it's still in the oven will overcook it.

  • Poultry (chicken, turkey, goose, duck)
  • Beef
  • Pork
  • Lamb
  • Ham (fresh and cured)
  • Steak
  • Fish (salmon, tuna)

Poultry

All whole birds go by the thigh, not the breast — the thigh is the last part to come up to temperature.

Fish

Most fish don't get a doneness spectrum below — cod, haddock, sea bass, sea bream, trout, mackerel, halibut, turbot, plaice, sole, swordfish, monkfish, red mullet, John Dory, herring and skate are all conventionally cooked right through in UK home cooking, no rare option worth codifying. General endpoint: {{ t.fish.general.endpoint }}, carryover {{ t.fish.general.carryover }}.

Sources: {{ t.fish.general.sources }}

Salmon and tuna are the two exceptions — both are routinely served under fully cooked, so both get a real doneness spectrum.
