Temperature ruler

The same numbers as the reference page, drawn on one scale instead of listed in five tables.

One axis, 40–100°C, shared by every protein — deliberately not one scale per protein. Letting each fill the full width looks tidier and throws away the only thing the drawing adds: that salmon is served at 52°C where a chicken thigh has to reach 74°C, and pulled pork doesn't give up until 96°C.

Everything, one scale
