--- layout: default --- {% comment %} ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── COCKTAIL PAGE — SKELETON. Deliberately not modelled on recipe.html. A food recipe is a PROCEDURE: ordered steps, prep and cook time split, a short-method toggle, main_ingredients triaged by the substitution test. A cocktail is closer to a FORMULA — ratio, glass, garnish, one line of method — and forcing it into food's decision-support shape is the specific mistake the architecture handover warns against. So this renders the two fields already known to be real (glass, garnish), the full untriaged ingredient list, and the body. It invents no schema beyond that. `title` is the only field it assumes. When the real recipes land, this is where their anatomy gets built — and the argument about what a cocktail page should show belongs there, over real drinks, the way main_ingredients was argued out over real recipes. ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────{% endcomment %}
