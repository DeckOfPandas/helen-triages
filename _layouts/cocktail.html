--- layout: default --- {% comment %} ───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────── COCKTAIL PAGE. Deliberately not modelled on recipe.html. A food recipe is a PROCEDURE: ordered steps, prep and cook time split, a short-method toggle, main_ingredients triaged by the substitution test. A cocktail is closer to a FORMULA — a spirit bill in fixed proportions, a glass, a garnish, a short build — and forcing it into food's decision-support shape is the specific mistake the architecture handover warns against. THE SCHEMA THIS RENDERS CAME OUT OF THREE REAL DRINKS (Julien Sorel, Sazerac, Cobra's Fang), the way food's came out of real recipes — not from general knowledge about cocktails. See HANDOVER's cocktails section for what each field is for, what is settled and what is still open. EMPTY ARRAY IS TRUTHY IN LIQUID. Only nil and false are falsy, so every list-gated section below tests `.size > 0` rather than the bare value. This is not hypothetical here: `garnish: []` is real, live data on sazerac.md (the drink genuinely has no garnish), and a bare truth test would draw a GARNISH heading over blank space. Same bug as the 49 food drafts with `notes: []` — see tests/test_site_config.py's test_list_sections_are_conditioned_on_size_not_truthiness. ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────{% endcomment %}
