There was one key at the top covering every chart below it, which is the arrangement that requires the reader to hold four definitions in their head while scrolling — and worse, to work out for themselves which of the four apply to whichever chart they are looking at. Poultry has no rested bar and no carryover; the overview has neither and no safety zone either; only three charts shade anything. A key that lists marks a chart doesn't contain is not a key, it's a glossary. Helen, and she is right that this is the scientific convention: a figure carries its own legend. Each chart now declares only what it draws, so a key entry appearing at all is itself information. {% endcomment %}
