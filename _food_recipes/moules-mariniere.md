method:
  - "Rinse the mussels under cold running water, discarding any that: a) are cracked, b) don't close under running water with enthusiastic rattling AND then continue not to close when tapped sharply on the side of the sink. Drain well."
  - "Melt the butter in a large, deep, lidded pan over a medium heat, then soften the shallots with salt for 3–4 mins."
  - "Turn up the heat and add the mussels, then the wine. Cover and cook for 4–5 mins, giving the pan an occasional shake until all the mussels have opened. Discard any that are clamped shut. Scatter over the parsley and season with black pepper, then turn everything over using a large serving spoon. Spoon over the cooking liquid to serve."
