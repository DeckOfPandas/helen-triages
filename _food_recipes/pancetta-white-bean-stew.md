  - "Fry the pancetta on medium heat for 5–6 mins until crispy and rendered. Remove with a slotted spoon to leave the fat."
  - "Fry the onion, celery and carrot slowly for 12–15 mins until golden. Add the garlic and cook for 1 min more."
  - "Add the beans, chicken stock and optional parmesan rind. Cook for about 15 mins on medium heat, stirring in the cavolo nero and cooked pancetta for the last 5 mins. Add the sherry vinegar, then taste and season."
  - "Sprinkle with fresh chives to serve."
