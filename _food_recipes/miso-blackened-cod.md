method_groups:
  - name: "Marinate (up to three days ahead)"
    steps:
      - "Bring the sake and mirin to a fast boil, then wait 20 seconds."
      - "Reduce the heat, add the miso and sugar, and whisk until dissolved."
      - "Cool to room temperature."
      - "Marinate the cod in a zip-lock bag for at least 48 hours, preferably 72."
  - name: "Grill the cod (on the day)"
    steps:
      - "Season the cod with salt and garlic powder."
      - "Grill for 8–12 mins, skin-side down (or smooth-side down) on a wire rack over a foil-lined baking tray, until the fish starts to char and flake. It's fine if the marinade burns a little."
  - name: "Make the sauce"
    steps:
      - "Meanwhile, bring the remainder of the marinade to the boil in a pan, add the soy sauce, stir for a minute, then reduce the heat."
      - "Thicken with cornflour."
