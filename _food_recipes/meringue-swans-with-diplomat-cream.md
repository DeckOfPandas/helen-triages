method_groups:
  - name: crème pâtissière
    steps:
      - "Heat the milk with the vanilla until steaming. Whisk the yolks with the sugar until pale, whisk in the cornflour, then pour the hot milk over slowly, whisking continuously. Return to the rinsed-out pan."
      - step: "Whisk over medium heat until it thickens and comes to a rolling boil. Let it bubble for a full minute."
        note: "If the cornflour isn't cooked out then the filling won't hold a neck up."
      - "Remove from the heat, and stir the butter in."
      - "Soak the gelatine in cold water for 5 mins, squeeze the water out, then whisk it into the cooling custard until completely dissolved."
      - step: "Press cling film onto the surface and chill until cold and set, at least 2 hours or overnight."
        note: "Any air contact will result in skin."
  - name: meringue
    steps:
      - "Line two trays with parchment. Fit a large star nozzle to one piping bag and a plain 3–4 mm nozzle to another."
      - "Heat the egg whites, caster sugar and lemon juice over a bain-marie, whisking constantly until it reaches 60°C and the sugar has dissolved (smooth between fingers)."
      - "Take off the heat, and whisk fast for 8–10 mins until glossy, stiff and cool. Whisk in the cornflour at the end."
      - step: "Pipe 10–12 elongated S shapes 8–10 cm tall with the small nozzle, tapering to a point at the head."
        note: "Necks will break, so best to pipe a few extras."
      - step: "Pipe teardrop bodies 7–8 cm long with the star nozzle: steady pressure for a rounded base, then release and pull away to taper the tail."
        note: "Or pipe flat bases with the small nozzle to avoid faffing with two piping sets."
      - "Pipe two wings per swan with the same nozzle, commas about half the size of the bodies."
      - step: "Bake at 100°C fan, wings for 1 hour, and the rest for about 1 hour 15 mins, until they lift cleanly off the parchment and sound hollow. Turn the oven off and leave everything inside to dry, another hour at least, ideally overnight."
        note: "Navigating shells vs chewy disc is determined by proper cooling."
  - name: assemble
    steps:
      - step: "Whisk the set custard smooth. Salt the cream, and whip to soft-medium peaks and fold it through in two additions. Chill in a piping bag with a star nozzle."
        note: "It's very hard to over-salt this kind of food."
      - "Cut an oval out of the top of each body with a small serrated knife, leaving a shell about 1 cm thick."
      - step: "Pipe cream into each body, mounded above the rim."
        note: "Or if common sense has been applied, pipe simple large balls of cream on your convenient flat bases."
      - "Press a neck in at the front and a wing onto each side, squeezing in pairs. Dot melted chocolate on the point of the neck for a beak. Dust with icing sugar just before serving."
method_short:
