method_groups:
  - name: "Brown the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Pat the chicken dry and season. Brown well on all sides in the butter and oil, about 10 mins in total. Remove from the casserole."
  - name: "Braise"
    steps:
      - "Pour off all but 1 tbsp fat. Soften the shallots for 8–10 mins (hardly any colour), then add the garlic for a minute."
      - "Stir in the flour and cook for a minute. Add the cognac and let it bubble away completely."
      - "Add the Sauternes, cider and vermouth and scrape up the stuck bits. Add the stock and herbs."
      - "Put the chicken back in, skin up. Bring to a simmer, cover and braise in the oven or on the hob at 150°C fan for 1¼–1¾ hours, until a knife slides easily into the thigh at the bone. Start checking consistency at 1¼ hours. Cockerel legs take longer than chicken legs."
  - name: "Mushrooms"
    steps:
      - "Meanwhile, fry the mushrooms hard in the butter until browned and their liquid has evaporated. Season."
  - name: "To finish"
    steps:
      - "Lift out the chicken and herbs. Reduce the liquid only a little because of the Sauternes sugar."
      - "Stir in the cream and mustard, then simmer until the sauce lightly coats a spoon. Add the mushrooms."
      - step: "Correct with lemon juice and salt."
        note: "Expect to need more lemon than you would think."
      - "Return the chicken to the sauce and warm through. Scatter with parsley."
