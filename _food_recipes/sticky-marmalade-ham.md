method:
  - step: "Heat the oven to 160°C fan."
    note: "Not just for convenience — the cook starts hotter."
  - "Place the ham in a large tin with 500 ml water. Cover loosely with greaseproof paper then foil, scrunched and sealed round the rim with room around the ham."
  - "Turn the oven down to 140°C fan and cook for about 5 hours."
  - "Boil the marmalade with the sugar and 50 ml water, then set aside to cool."
  - step: "Once the ham has cooled a little, prise the rind from the fat, keeping as much fat on the leg as possible. Drain off most of the liquid, leaving a little in the tin."
    note: "The liquid makes excellent ham stock."
  - "Turn the oven up to 180°C fan."
  - "Score the fat into large 4-cm diamonds. Brush a third of the glaze over, pushing it into the cuts. Press a clove into the corner of each diamond."
  - "Sit the ham in the tin on a trivet, or some halved carrots. Roast uncovered 45–50 mins, brushing with glaze a few more times, until caramelised and glossy. Carve hot or cold."
