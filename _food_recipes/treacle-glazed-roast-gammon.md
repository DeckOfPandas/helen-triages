method_groups:
  - name: "gammon"
    steps:
      - "Drain the gammon and place in a stockpot or large saucepan. Cover with cold water, and bring slowly to the boil, skimming off any froth and scum. Then lower the heat and simmer for a few mins before carefully pouring off the water along with any more froth and scum."
      - "Add the onion, carrot, leek, cinnamon stick, bay leaves and peppercorns to the pot, then pour in the stout and top up with cold water until the gammon is fully submerged."
      - "Simmer, partially covered, for 2½ hrs over a gentle heat, skimming off any scum as it cooks and topping up with boiling water if needed."
      - "Remove the gammon from the pot and rest until cool enough to handle."
      - "Peel the skin off, leaving an even 5 mm layer of fat, then score the fat in a criss-cross diamond pattern at 1.5-cm intervals, studding each diamond with a clove."
      - "Transfer to a roasting tray."
  - name: "treacle glaze"
    steps:
      - "Mix together all the glaze ingredients and brush half over the gammon. Roast for at 170°C fan for 15 mins, then pour over the rest of the glaze and roast for a further 10–15 mins, basting every 5 mins. Remove from the oven, give the meat a final basting with the pan juices, and leave to rest before carving into thin slices."
