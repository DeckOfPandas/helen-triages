method:
  - "Cut the pin bones out in a central V to create two smaller fillets each. Rub with the oil and season generously with pepper."
  - "Stir the fennel with the beetroot, cucumber, apple, lemon juice, and yoghurt, and mix well, then scatter over the chopped dill, season with black pepper and toss lightly."
  - "Fry the fish skin-side down 2–3 mins over medium-high heat, then flip and cook the other side for 30 seconds."
  - "Serve the fillets on top of the salad with the reserved dill fronds and lemon wedges, squeezing some lemon juice over."
