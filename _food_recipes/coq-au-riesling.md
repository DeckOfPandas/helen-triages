method_groups:
  - name: "Brown the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Pat the chicken dry and season. Brown well on all sides in the butter and oil in a casserole, in batches if needed, about 10 mins in total. Set aside."
  - name: "Braise"
    steps:
      - "Pour off all but about 1 tbsp fat. Soften the shallots for 8–10 mins (not much colour), then add the garlic for a minute."
      - "Stir in the flour and cook for a minute. Add the cognac and let it bubble away."
      - "Add the Riesling and scrape up the stuck bits, then add the stock and herbs."
      - "Put the chicken back in, skin up. Bring to a simmer, cover and braise in the oven or on the hob at 150°C fan for 1¼–1¾ hours, until a knife slides easily into the thigh at the bone. Start checking consistency at 1¼ hours. Cockerel legs take longer than chicken legs."
  - name: "Mushrooms"
    steps:
      - "Meanwhile, fry the mushrooms hard in the butter until browned and their liquid has evaporated. Season."
  - name: "To finish"
    steps:
      - "Lift out the chicken and remove the herbs. Reduce the liquid on the hob by about a third."
      - "Stir in the crème fraîche and simmer until it lightly coats a spoon. Add the mushrooms, lemon and seasoning."
      - "Return the chicken to the sauce and warm through. Scatter with parsley."
