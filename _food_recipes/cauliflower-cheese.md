method:
  - "Boil the cauliflower florets for 4–5 mins until just tender. Drain and refresh in iced water, then drain again and lay out in an oven dish."
  - step: "Make the roux: melt the butter gently, then stir in the flour and mustard powder for a smooth paste. Then add the milk a little at a time, stirring/whisking continuously until completely smooth and almost shiny."
    note: "Each of these stages takes longer than one might consider reasonable."
  - "Bring to the boil, then simmer for a few minutes, stirring continuously, until it thickens. Remove from the heat, add the English mustard powder, then any other flavourings (curry powder, Worcestershire sauce etc). Season with salt and pepper."
  - Grate the three cheeses together. Add half to the roux pan and stir until fully melted. Pour the sauce over the cauliflower.
  - "Add the breadcrumbs and thyme to the other half of the cheese, then tip evenly over the cauliflower dish."
  - Bake at 180°C fan for about 20 mins until the topping is crisp and the sauce is bubbling.

    * Bake 180°C fan 15–20 mins
