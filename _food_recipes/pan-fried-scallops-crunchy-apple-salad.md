  - name: "apple salad"
    steps:
      - "Mix the apple matchsticks with the lamb's lettuce, and season with salt and pepper."

  - name: "scallops"
    steps:
      - "Lay the scallops out on a board, and pat as dry as possible with kitchen paper."
      - "Heat a large non-stick frying pan over a high heat until smoking hot, no oil yet."
      - "Immediately before frying, add 1 tbsp oil to the pan, and season one side of each scallop with salt and pepper."
      - "Add the scallops to the pan one by one, seasoned side down, working clockwise, then fry for 1–2 mins until golden brown."
      - "Season the other side, flip each one over in the same order they went in, and repeat."
      - "Squeeze over the lemon juice and shake the pan."
      - "When the scallops are cooked, tip the contents of the pan onto a plate lined with kitchen paper to soak up excess oil."
      - "Divide the salad between two plates and arrange the scallops around each pile. Garnish with the remaining lemon zest and serve immediately."
