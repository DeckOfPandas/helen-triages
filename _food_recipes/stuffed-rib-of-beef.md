method_groups:
  - name: "stuffing"
    steps:
      - "Bring the beef to room temperature, about an hour."
      - "For the stuffing, soften the onion in a dash of oil in a heavy sauté pan over medium heat with salt and pepper. Add the mushrooms and cook until tender, then the garlic for 2 mins, then the spinach in batches."
      - "Once the spinach has wilted completely, take off the heat and chop finely. Cool slightly in a bowl, then stir in the breadcrumbs and herbs. Season, then stir in the egg yolk to bind."
  - name: "beef"
    steps:
      - "Lay the beef on a board, ribs to the right. Insert a sharp knife on the left about 3 cm in and cut a long incision just above the ribs, two-thirds into the joint, separating meat from bone but leaving it attached along the bottom."
      - "Ease the meat apart and push the stuffing in. Any excess goes in a small covered dish, baked for the last 10–15 mins. Tie string horizontally round the meat and vertically between the ribs."
      - "Mix the mustard powder with salt, pepper and enough olive oil to make a thin paste, and rub it into the meat."
      - "Put the carrots and parsnips in the tin, drizzle with olive oil, and sit the beef on top. Roast at 230°C fan for 15 mins to brown."
      - "Drop to 180°C fan and roast for about 15 mins per 450 g, plus 15 mins for rare or 20–30 for medium rare (check [internal temperatures chart](#doneness)). Take the vegetables out after 35 mins."
      - "Cover loosely with foil and rest for at least 20 mins, up to 45."
      - "Return the vegetables to the oven for 15 mins to finish."
      - "Untie the beef, slice away the rib bones, and carve. Serve with the stuffing and vegetables."
