method_groups:
  - name: "Make the dry rub"
    steps:
      - "Toast the Szechuan peppercorns for the rub in a dry pan for 2 mins until fragrant, then grind finely in a mortar and pestle or spice grinder."
      - "Mix with the black peppercorns, garlic powder, onion powder and salt."
  - name: "Season the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Pat the spatchcocked chicken completely dry with kitchen paper, then rub all over with the sesame or vegetable oil."
      - "Apply the dry rub generously, working some of it under the skin as well as over it."
  - name: "Roast"
    steps:
      - "Heat the oven to 200°C fan. Lay the chicken flat, skin-side up, on a wire rack set over a baking tray."
      - "Roast undisturbed for 30 mins to crisp the skin and render the fat."
  - name: "Glaze and finish"
    steps:
      - "While the chicken is in its first roast, simmer all the glaze ingredients in a small saucepan for 8–10 mins until syrupy."
      - "Brush the chicken generously with the glaze, then return to the oven for a further 15–20 mins, brushing with more glaze every 5 mins, until the thickest part of the thigh reads 74°C on a probe thermometer."
      - "Rest for 10 mins before carving."
