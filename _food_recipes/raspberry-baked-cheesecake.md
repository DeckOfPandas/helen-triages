method:

  - "Beat the cream cheese and sugar together, then add the beaten eggs a little at a time. Add the flour and lemon zest, then fold through the raspberries."
  - "Spoon the mixture into the cake tin, tapping it against the work surface to remove any bubbles and help the raspberries rise up from the bottom. Bake at 160°C fan for 35–40 mins, until set at the edges but still slightly wobbly in the middle."
  - "Cool completely before removing from the tin."
