method:
  - "Mix the flour, baking powder and rosemary with generous salt."
  - "Beat the egg with the milk, then make a well in the flour mixture and beat in the egg mixture slowly."
  - "Whisk in the melted butter to make a thick, smooth batter, then fridge while you do the rest."
  - "Sauté the butternut squash in butter for about 10 mins until tender, turning up the heat a little at the end to brown the edges."
  - "Carefully fold the squash and cheese into the batter, being careful not to squash the squash."
  - "Fry in small pancakes with butter, 3-4 mins per side."
