method:
  - "Fry the onion with a pinch of salt slowly, about 15 mins, until caramelised."
  - "Prep the runner beans: trim, de-string, and cut on the diagonal."
  - "Add the garlic and anchovies to the onion. Cook for a few minutes, until the anchovies dissolve and the garlic is cooked but not browned."
  - "Add the runner beans, tomatoes, oregano leaves, vinegar, sugar and some salt. Cover and cook for 15 mins over a low heat, stirring often, until the tomatoes break down and the beans soften."
  - "Add the cannellini beans, then half a can of water. Cook uncovered for 10 mins, until reduced a little. Check the seasoning and stir in the chopped basil leaves."
  - "Serve with good olive oil."
