method:
  - "Tip the finely sliced onions into a large deep pan or roasting tray, season generously and toss to coat with a little oil."
  - step: "Thoroughly dry the pork, score the skin and fat in a diamond pattern if not already scored, rub the sea salt into the cuts, then place the pork on top of the onions and roast in a very high oven for 20 mins until beginning to blister."
    tip: "Make sure you don't cut the skin all the way through — keeping the fat inside is what makes the crackling."
  - "Finely slice the potatoes, scatter over the thyme, garlic and rosemary, and season well."
  - "After 20 mins, turn the oven down to QQ°C. Remove the pork and brush with mustard, avoiding the skin. Stir the onions, add the potatoes, stir to coat, pour in the chicken stock, then return the pork on top and roast for another 40 mins."
  - "Scatter gruyère over the potatoes and grill on high until the pork skin is blistered and crackling and the potatoes are golden — 60°C internally on a meat thermometer."
  - "Slice the pork and serve with the potatoes and plenty of pan sauce."
