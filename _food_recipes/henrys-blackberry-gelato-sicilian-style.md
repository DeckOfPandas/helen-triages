method:
  - "Blend the blackberries with the sugar and the lemon juice until smooth. Pass through a fine mesh strainer, pressing with a spatula, to remove all the seeds — to get about 300 ml of purée. Set aside."
  - "Whisk together the sugar, cornflour and salt in a saucepan. Gradually whisk in the milk and cream until smooth with no lumps."
  - "Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, until it just starts to bubble and thickens slightly to a light custard consistency (just about coating the back of a spoon). This takes about 6–8 mins — do not let it boil hard."
  - "Remove from the heat and stir in the blackberry purée. Cool to room temperature, then cover and refrigerate until fully cold."
  - "Freeze however you do it — you want a dense texture, not fluffy, so don't over-aerate."
