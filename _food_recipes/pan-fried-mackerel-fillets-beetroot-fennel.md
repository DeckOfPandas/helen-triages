  - name: "Mackerel"
    steps:
      - "Cut the pin bones out in a central V to create two smaller fillets. Rub with the oil and season generously with black pepper."
      - "Fry skin-side down 2–3 mins over medium-high heat, then fry the other side for 30 seconds."
  - name: "Make the salad"
    steps:
      - "Stir the fennel with the beetroot, cucumber, apple, lemon juice, and yoghurt, mix well, then scatter over the chopped dill. Season with black pepper and toss lightly."
  - name: "to serve"
    steps:
      - "Serve the fillets on top of the salad with the reserved dill fronds and lemon wedges, squeezing some lemon juice over."
