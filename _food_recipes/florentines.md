method:
  - "Find baking sheets, some greaseproof or silicone paper, and a 2½ inch (6.5 cm) pastry cutter."
  - "Melt 2 tbsp butter together with the sugar and flour in a heavy saucepan over very low heat, stirring until melted and completely smooth."
  - "Gradually add the cream, stirring continuously, then add all the remaining ingredients except the chocolate and stir thoroughly. Remove from the heat and set aside to cool."
  - "Brush the baking sheets with melted butter, dust with flour and tap off the excess."
  - "Spoon teaspoonfuls of the mixture onto the prepared sheets, spaced about an inch apart to allow for spreading, then flatten each with the back of the spoon."
  - "Bake on a high shelf for about 10 mins, until the edges just tinge brown. If they spread too much, use a greased pastry cutter to ease them back into neat rounds. Bake for a further 5 mins until golden, remove from the oven, then leave to harden on the sheets for 3 mins before transferring to a wire rack to cool."
  - "Melt the chocolate in a bowl over barely simmering water, then spread it in a layer roughly 3 mm thick on sheets of greaseproof paper."
  - "Place the cooled Florentines base-down on the warm melted chocolate and leave until cold and set. Using the same cutter, cut out each biscuit with its new chocolate base (the trimmings can be peeled off the paper and melted again)."
  - "Spread a little of the melted trimmings over the set chocolate base on each Florentine and, just before it sets, mark a wavy pattern with a fork."
  - "Leave to set again, then pack in alternating rows, fruit-side and chocolate-side up, in boxes or tins."
