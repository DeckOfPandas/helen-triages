method_groups:
  - name: Char the asparagus
    steps:
      - "Toss the asparagus in olive oil and salt on a baking tray, arranging in a single layer."
      - "Grill on a high heat, 5–7.5 cm from the grill, 4–6 mins, until the asparagus is lightly charred and just tender."
  - name: Make the dressing
    steps:
      - "Combine the yoghurt, sour cream, basil, chives, Parmesan, lemon zest, lemon juice, mint, sugar, ½ tsp salt and 3 tbsp water. The dressing should be thick but spoonable — loosen with water if needed."
  - name: Assemble
    steps:
      - "Spoon a tablespoon of the dressing into each lettuce or chicory leaf and arrange them on a serving platter. Scatter the charred asparagus among the leaves, and drizzle with olive oil."
