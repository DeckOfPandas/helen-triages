method_groups:
  - name: "Roast the duck"
    steps: 
      - step: "Set aside the giblets and pat the duck as dry as possible. Prick the skin all over, particularly in the leg fat, then sit the bird on a roasting tray and salt comprehensively."
      - step: "Roast at 220°C fan for 20 mins, then drop the oven to 180°C fan and roast for a further approximately 50 mins, basting halfway through."
  - name: "Make the duck stock"
    steps:
      - step: "Discard the liver from the giblets (to avoid making the sauce bitter). Fry the neck and heart over medium heat until browned all over, then add the peppercorns, star anise, thyme and 500 ml water."
        note: "If short on time, just add less water, because this sauce definitely takes its time to cook down."
      - step: "Simmer vigorously for about 20 mins until reduced to about 100 ml. Strain through a fine sieve into a jug and discard the solids."
  - name: "Make the rest of the sauce"
    steps: 
      - step: "Juice two of the oranges to give around 150 ml juice. Slice the peel from the third, then remove as much pith as possible, then slice the rest of the peel into 1-mm strips. Segment the whole orange, adding any extra juice to the bowl to the rest of the juice."
      - step: "Simmer the sugar and vinegar until syrupy, a few mins, then add the wine, duck stock and orange juice. Bring to a simmer and cook until reduced by two thirds, about 20 mins. Add the strips of orange peel, then leave to reduce further until glossy."
      - step: "Keep warm to serve."
        note: "If there's going to be a gap, sieve the orange peel out rather than leaving it standing, then add it back at the end, to avoid bitterness."
  - name: "Assemble, garnish and serve"
    steps: 
      - step: "Once cooked, leave the duck to rest for 20 mins."
      - step: "Carve the duck. Finish with the blood orange segments and some sauce, with the rest of the sauce on the side."
