method:
  - "Combine the marinade ingredients with 2 tbsp water in a large bowl. Pour half of the marinade into a smaller bowl, then add the salmon to the large bowl. Coat thoroughly."
  - "Slice the sweet potatoes into ½-cm slices. Place on a baking tray and toss to coat with a little oil. Arrange in an even layer, then bake for 15–20 mins until almost tender."
  - step: "Add the salmon, broccoli and pak choi. Sprinkle over the sesame seeds, drizzle over any marinade left in the large bowl and return to the oven for 12 mins."
    tip: "Making double the marinade gives you extra for drizzling at the end."
  - "Serve immediately, drizzling the salmon with the reserved miso marinade from the smaller bowl. Top with the spring onions."
