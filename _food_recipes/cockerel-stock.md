method_groups:
  - name: "Simmer"
    steps:
      - step: "Put the bones and giblets in a large pot and cover with cold water."
        note: "Starting cold gives a clearer stock."
      - "Bring slowly to a bare simmer over a medium heat. Skim off the scum as it rises, for the first 15–20 mins."
      - step: "Add the aromatics. Keep it at a bare simmer, with just the odd bubble breaking the surface, uncovered or partly covered, for 3–4 hours. Top up with water if the bones are exposed."
        note: "Boiling makes it cloudy and greasy."
  - name: "Strain and cool"
    steps:
      - "Strain through a fine sieve, or a sieve lined with muslin, without pressing the solids."
      - "Cool it quickly: split it between wide containers and stand them in a sink of cold water. Get it into the fridge within 1–2 hours."
      - "Once chilled, lift off the set fat and keep it for roasting potatoes."
