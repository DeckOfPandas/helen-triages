method:
  - "Mix the grated pear, sugar and aromatics in a bowl or jar. Cover and refrigerate for 24–48 hours, stirring once or twice, until the sugar has fully dissolved into a fragrant syrup. It will go brown, which doesn't matter."
  - "Strain through a muslin-lined sieve, pressing the pulp hard. Scrape any undissolved sugar into the syrup."
  - "Whisk in the vinegar. Bottle and fridge."
  - "Leave it a week before using if possible, because the vinegar mellows noticeably. It keeps for months."
