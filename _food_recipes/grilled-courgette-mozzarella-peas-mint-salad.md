method:
  - "Cover the peas with boiling water for 3 mins, then drain well."
  - "Toss the courgettes with 1 tbsp oil and plenty of salt. Griddle cut-side down 4–5 mins, until grill-marked, then flip for 3 mins more. Tip back into the bowl with the remaining oil, the sherry vinegar and the peas. Toss and cover 2–3 mins."
  - "Divide the pea shoots between the two plates. Season the pea and courgette mixture, toss through the mint, and tip it over the pea shoots. Scatter over the mozzarella and drizzle with any leftover dressing."
  - "Serve warm crusty bread alongside."
