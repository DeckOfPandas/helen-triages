method_groups:
  - name: "Morels"
    steps:
      - "Soak the morels in 250 ml warm water for 30 mins. Lift them out and rinse well (because they're gritty)."
      - "Strain the soaking liquid through muslin or kitchen paper and retain."
  - name: "Brown the chicken"
    steps:
      - "Season the legs and brown gently in the butter, to golden rather than dark. Set aside."
  - name: "Braise"
    steps:
      - "Soften the shallots in the same pan for 8 mins without colouring."
      - "Add the vin jaune and bubble for 2 mins. Add the morel liquid and the stock."
      - "Return the legs, cover and braise in the oven or on the hob at 150°C fan for 1¼–1¾ hours, until tender at the bone."
      - "Meanwhile, sauté the morels in the butter for 5 mins and season."
  - name: "To finish"
    steps:
      - "Lift out the legs and reduce the liquid by half. Add the crème fraîche and morels, and simmer until the sauce coats a spoon."
      - "Take off the heat, and stir in the reserved vin jaune. Return the legs and serve."
