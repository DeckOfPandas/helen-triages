method_groups:
  - name: "Brine"
    steps:
      - "Take the legs off if not already done. Cut away the lower part of the backbone with kitchen scissors and keep it, with the neck, for future [cockerel stock](../cockerel-stock/)."
      - "Pat the crown dry. Weigh it and salt it all over with 8 g fine salt per kg, underside included."
      - "Put it on a rack, uncovered, in the main fridge for at least 3 hours, better 24–48 hours."
  - name: "Roast"
    steps:
      - "Don't rinse the crown; just pat it dry."
      - "Loosen the skin over each breast with fingers, push the butter underneath and smooth it out from the outside."
      - "Sit the crown skin up on the onions and carrots in a roasting tin, with the lemon halves and thyme alongside. Place an in-oven temperature probe."
      - "Roast at 220°C fan for 20 mins, then the rest at 160°C fan, until the breast reads 63°C, about 1–1¼ hours more. If the skin colours too fast, tent loosely with foil."
      - "Rest on a board, under loosely tented foil, for 30 mins, to 66–68°C."
  - name: "Gravy"
    steps:
      - "Spoon most of the fat out of the tin. Put the tin on the hob, or scrape everything into a pan. Stir in the flour, and cook for a minute."
      - "Add the wine, scrape up the stuck bits and bubble until reduced by half. Add the stock and simmer for 5–10 mins, then squeeze in the roasted lemon."
      - "Strain, add the resting juices, and taste before adding any salt (because of the brining)."
  - name: "Serve"
    steps:
      - "Take each breast off the bone whole, then slice it across the grain."
      - "Retain the carcass with the neck, tailbone and giblets, for [cockerel stock](../cockerel-stock/). These can go straight in the freezer."
