method:
  - "Finely shred the green spring onion tops, and place in cold water."
  - "Combine 1 tbsp sesame oil with 1 tbsp each of the mirin and miso, and a splash of the lime juice. Place the sausages and cabbage wedges into a large oven tray and brush over the glaze, coating both sides of the cabbage wedges. Roast at 180°C fan for 20 mins."
  - "Combine the finely sliced white spring onion ends with 1 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp miso, 2 tbsp mirin, and the rest of the lime juice, then add the ginger, garlic, tahini and rice vinegar. Add one can of butter beans, then the other can, drained. Add water, using ⅓ of an empty 400 g butter bean tin as a measure, and stir to combine."
  - "After the 20 mins roasting, remove the sausages and cabbage from the roasting tin, then add the butter bean mixture. Nestle the sausages and cabbage wedges on top, and cook for 20–25 mins more until the liquid has reduced and the sausages are browned. Drain the shredded spring onion tops and scatter these over, then serve."
