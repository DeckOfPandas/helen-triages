What colour is a hit?

The headings are the same in every option below: electric absinthe over deep absinthe, stepped, identical on all five. That is the stability you liked, and fixing it is what makes the rest a fair comparison.

What varies is what a match wears on a card — the ingredient band, the mood chip, and the goodness mark. Four cards per option, carrying deliberately different amounts: one with no matches at all, one ordinary, one busy, and one where nearly everything is lit.

First: the headings, two ways

You said “electric absinthe and deep absinthe” and that the stability was the attraction. Left is one identical mark on all five. Right keeps five green shades with deep absinthe as the shared lower bar — still stable underneath, still differentiated on top. Both at real size and spacing.

3 — hits carry no hue at all

Mine. The band and the lit chip already say “matched” by their shape; the colour is not carrying anything the position is not. 4 — everything absinthe, including the goodness mark

Mine, and the honest end of “shades of green for everything”. One hue on the whole page. Worth looking at precisely because it is the version most likely to fail: §9.13 keeps the goodness mark and the matched ingredient far apart on purpose, since those two are the only places colour carries meaning alone. Here they are the same hue, and the question is whether the square's border and position do the work its colour used to.
