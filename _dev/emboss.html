That page reproduced the treatment by hand, and got it wrong in a way that misled Helen for a whole exchange: it applied the STROKE only, with no shadow, so Courier Prime looked flat and the fault looked like the font's. The emboss is stroke AND a two-copy directional shadow, and a reproduction is a second thing to keep in step with the first. So this page carries no styling of its own for the effect. The headings below are ordinary h1/h2/h3, which pick up shared/_base.scss's real rule through the real cascade with the real fonts loaded. What the dials do is INJECT AN OVERRIDE into #tune-sheet — so what you are looking at is the site's own treatment with different numbers in it, not an imitation of it.
