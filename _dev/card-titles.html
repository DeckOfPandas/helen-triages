Issue #469. ROUND 2, 2026-09-01. Round 1 offered four lettering treatments and Helen chose one in one look: "the boldness of the two near-whites with no softening shadow is exactly what we need. Hugging the text is right. No tilt here." That is the single-pair bevel, hugging, straight -- and it is now FIXED on every row here rather than being one of the options. The other three lettering candidates, the tilt variant and all the explanatory prose are deleted, per the comparison-switch convention: once she picks, the losers and the switch go. THE QUESTION THIS ROUND IS BIGGER, and it is hers: "is this the road we want to go down design-wise???" So the page stopped being about lettering and became about the CARD. Her diagnosis of what to look at is the brief: - the glass panel "is the part that most jars with the new tape" - "the underlining and chip highlighting actually works fantastically well, which is a good hint we're doing something plausible" -- so the pink band language is load-bearing and appears on every row unchanged - a bolder reimagining may keep only two things: the pink and the home absinthe THE PINK IS FOOD'S HOT MAGENTA HERE, NOT COCKTAILS' COSMOPOLITAN. Helen, 2026-09-01: "could we please use the hot magenta from the food site as our pink? I just can't get away from loving it, and honestly that is my personal brand both at home and at work." #FF00C8 rather than #F127A7, applied to every pink thing on this page -- the lit chips, the chosen pool buttons, the ingredient bands and the matched mood chips -- so the question is asked of the whole page at once rather than one element at a time. It is page-local: making it real is a change to _sass/cocktails/_palette.scss, which is a different commit and should follow her looking, not precede it. THE DEEP AND THE WASH ARE DERIVED, NOT INVENTED. #C4009A is food's own $color-bright-magenta-dark (darken 12%), measured at 5.32:1 against this site's paper, so it clears AA as text. #FBE2F5 is a 10% tint of the bare hue on paper, the same construction as every other -wash in the palette. If this is adopted, both want solving properly rather than copying from here. NO PROSE ON THE PAGE, at Helen's ask: "remove everything on the page other than the mock filters and the cards. No extra visual distractions." Each row carries a two-character tag and nothing else, so she can name what she likes. Everything that used to be written on the page is written here instead. WHAT IS REAL AND WHAT IS NOT. Every card is a real `.drink-card` with the real glass panel and compression curve, real `.drink-card-hit` bands, real `.drink-card-mood.is-match` chips and the real goodness mark, built from the index's own Liquid. The filter block is the index's own markup with one invented state written in -- MOOD tiki, HAS TO HAVE lime juice, LEAVE OUT pineapple juice -- so every lit control and every banded word is the honest consequence of one state. Nothing clicks. THE FOUR DRINKS ARE REAL and chosen for title length: 8, 19, 24 and 48 characters. The Bellini is the collection's longest and already ellipsises on the live index; a tape breaks `text-overflow`, so it clips instead. Left visible, because it is what would actually happen. ROWS. 1 the settled tape title, everything else exactly as shipped 2a-2d the glass panel, four ways 3a-3d bolder reimaginings of the whole card TAPE SHAPES ARE DETERMINISTIC by card position, so a reload gives the same comparison and a drink wears the same shape on every row. Helen expects to draw more shapes -- `scripts/generate_tape.py` is the tool and §13.9 is the procedure.
