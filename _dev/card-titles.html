Issue #469. ROUND 6, 2026-09-01, and it is a subtraction. Helen: "Let's do less." Six stages, one variable moving at a time. WHAT SHE ASKED FOR: the original; E2a without lifting the colour of the tape; E4c without lifting the tape; all with the lettering vertically centred on the tape; then E2a with a top edge "but much much less". THE CENTRING WAS A REAL FAULT AND IT IS MEASURED, NOT NUDGED. Every tape SVG insets its black polygon 16.47% from the top of its viewBox and 12.94% from the bottom -- identical across all seven files, checked rather than assumed -- so the BAND's centre sits 1.76% of the box height BELOW the BOX's centre. The lettering was flex-centred on the box, which is the wrong target: it was landing high on the visible tape by about a pixel at card size. Capitals add a little more of the same, sitting slightly high inside their own line box. So `--tape-optical` moves the lettering down by 0.048em, spent as asymmetric padding so the tape's total height does not change. 0.028em of that is the band offset and is exact; the rest is the cap-height correction and is an ESTIMATE -- Courier Prime ships here as woff2 only and there is no font parser in this environment, so its real cap height and ascent could not be read. If the lettering still sits a hair high or low, that one value is the knob and nothing else needs touching. AND THIS IS WHY E4b IS GONE. Helen guessed it: "maybe vertically centered means we wouldn't need E4b." E4b gave the tape more vertical padding so its rim would stop crowding the letters -- treating a CENTRING fault as a SPACING one. With the letters actually centred there is nothing to make room for, so the fix is deleted rather than kept as a second dial. THE TAPE IS AT ITS OWN COLOUR THROUGHOUT. Rounds 3-5 brightened it; she has taken that off. The consequence is the thing to look at: on a lifted ground with a dark card, a true-black tape is very nearly the card's own colour, so it has almost no fill to be seen by. What is left is its EDGE -- which is exactly why she asked for a top edge next, and why the ladder below runs down to almost nothing rather than up. THE STAGES, one variable each: O the original -- page darker than the card, tape darkest, no edge G the ground lifted and the card dark, tape unlifted, no edge L G plus the 0.18 top edge, which is E4c without the tape lift T1 G plus 0.10 T2 G plus 0.06 T3 G plus 0.03 Deleted with the losers: the ground ladder's second step, the worn roll, the roomy padding, and every C and D treatment. Rounds 2-5 hold them. Four real drinks, 8 / 19 / 24 / 48 characters.
