Glasses on cards

Every glass in glasses.yml, drawn in a real card panel at the real scale, tallest first. The tallest is {{ tallest_mm | round }} mm and it is the reference every other glass is measured against — so it is always 1.0 and always fills the panel, whatever it happens to be. Unused glasses are drawn too, greyed in the label: tallest_mm is counted over all_icons, not over the glasses in use, so one of those can set the scale for the whole set without appearing on a single card.

Shape

Both curves lift the SHORT end. Neither moves the tallest glass, which is 1.0 by construction — that is what headroom is for, and why the two compose rather than competing.

Dials

The controls open on exactly what ships: compressed at k = 0.50, headroom 1.00. Anything that moves is something you moved. The headroom dial is relative to the shipped --card-glass-scale, which this page reads live — so 1.00 follows that value rather than pinning a number here, and the two cannot drift apart. display_scale is empty since the curve replaced it, so the cheats box now changes nothing until a glass earns a line.
