Narrow card layouts

Three candidate layouts for the cocktail index card below 400px, from issue #483. Each frame below is a real viewport of the stated width — an iframe gets its own layout width, so these are honest at 360px even though the page around them is not.

Helen, 2026-08-27: “let’s try glasses on top, info below then, for the smaller screens. Or maybe glass / title on top, details below.”

360px is the width that matters most. It is the dominant Android portrait width; 375px covers the iPhone SE, 8 and 13 mini; 390px is the iPhone 14/15. 320px is the historical floor and largely gone — it is here as a stress test, not a target. At 360px the card’s text column is about 157px in the original layout, roughly eighteen characters of Courier Prime.
The content got harder while these were being built. Cards now show each ingredient’s category rather than the source’s word for it (#501/#531), so the longest ingredient string on a card is now 44 characters — Grand Marnier or other cognac orange liqueur. Judge the layouts on that, not on what was there before.
