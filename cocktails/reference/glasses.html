A SPEC, NOT AN INVENTORY. #295 asked for "a dictionary of glasses I own with their volumes"; Helen, 2026-09-28: "I don't want inventory, just spec. I might buy new glasses (okay I certainly will). The 'right' glasses might be dirty. I might have more guests than 'right' glasses. Basically, as usual with this site, let's offer rules, then I'll happily break them in the kitchen." So this is what each glass type holds ON THE MARKET, and nothing about the cupboard. HER BRIEF, 2026-09-29, AND IT IS THE WHOLE PAGE: "show all the glasses together, at relative scale, giving the range and mean ml for each (given the medians are similar we don't need to give those). Don't show the heights. Don't show anything else on the page body. Style GLASSES like a heading. And let's try the glasses in green." So: no intro, no notes, no sources line, no links to drinks. The sources live in `_data/cocktails/glass_survey.yml`, one per product, for anyone who asks. EVERY NUMBER IS A LOOKUP. The drawing is `_includes/icons/glasses/`, its height `heights_mm` (the surveyed median, or Helen's override), the ml `typical_ml` -- all generated from the survey by scripts/glass_survey_stats.py. Nothing here is typed. RELATIVE SCALE IS `_dev/glasses.html` SECTION 2's, the view Helen asked for by name ("I love the side by side view on the ref page, the version based on real glasses"): each glass's real height over the tallest real glass, every glass standing on one baseline per row. ALPHABETICAL, BY THE NAME PRINTED. It shipped ordered by capacity, shot to punch bowl; Helen, 2026-09-29: "Please draw them in alphabetical order so the visual effect is nice and messy." Each row is a "name|type|drawing" string sorted as a string, which is alphabetical by the name and puts `mug` before `mule mug`. EVERY ml IS ROUNDED TO THE NEAREST 10 ON THE PAGE -- Helen, same day: "Please round all ml measurements to the nearest 10 ml." The data keeps whole ml; the rounding is how the page SAYS them, so it happens here and nowhere else. EACH DRAWING STANDS ITS BASE OVER ITS LABEL, not its box. `base_centre` in glasses.yml is where the base sits as a fraction of the drawing's width, and `--glass-shift` moves the drawing by minus that: "the text under the normal mug is way off to the right, and the text for mule mug and pineapple are slightly right of centre." The handle is what had pulled the box right. A TYPE THAT BORROWS A DRAWING STILL GETS ITS OWN ENTRY, because it is its own size: `survey_only_types` in glasses.yml. The mule mug was the one such type until 2026-10-02, when Helen drew it its own mug; the map is empty now and the loop below is kept for the next one. THE NAME is the spelling a drink writes -- the first `icons:` key for that drawing that `canonical_glasses` does not correct -- so the page says "double old fashioned" and "brandy glass", exactly the words on a drink page.
