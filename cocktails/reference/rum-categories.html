WHY THIS PAGE PASSES #459 ("being an encyclopaedia of drinks sounds like busywork and it's not for me") when a bare list of eleven category names would fail it: the bottles column. #501 moved a question from the page to the reader — cards stopped naming bottles and started naming categories, so "which of mine is a Demerara rum?" had nowhere to be answered. This is the answer, and it is the column carrying the justification, not the decoration. EVERY NAME ON THIS PAGE IS A LOOKUP, NOT A LITERAL. The categories are `rum_styles`, the short names are `card_names`, the bottles are the entries in `bottles.yml` whose `generic` is that category, the retired words and their reasons are `retired_rum_styles`, and the Ceylon arrack note is `family_less`. Nothing here restates a fact the data already holds, which is the failure mode #314's own closing comment fell into twice (a rule stated once, as a list, going stale with nothing noticing). THE BOTTLES MEAN BOTTLES HELEN WOULD REACH FOR, NOT BOTTLES THAT QUALIFY — settled 2026-08-27 by the case that separates the two: Lemon Hart 151 clears the Demerara appellation and is deliberately absent. `bottles.yml`'s own header carries the ruling; this page inherits it by reading that file and must never be "completed" from anywhere else. A CATEGORY WITH NO BOTTLE IS CORRECT AND IS NOT A GAP TO FILL. `overproof Demerara rum` has none since El Dorado 151 moved to `not_reached_for` on 2026-09-05 ("I don't own it, I just wanted to"). The generic stays because a drink still asks for it. Do not repopulate the row. THE WORDING OF EVERY LINE OF COPY HERE IS A PLACEHOLDER AND IS HELEN'S TO WRITE — the same status as the units line (#753) and the bitters caveat (#713). That covers the intro, the three column headings and the two sentences introducing the characters and the retired words. What each must CONVEY is fixed; the words are not. Do not polish them into house voice. RUM ONLY, deliberately, which is the issue's own scope. `bottles.yml` stopped being rum-only on 2026-08-30 and now holds 127 bottles across every family, so the same page shape would render gin or agave with no new machinery. That is a decision for Helen, not an extension to assume. {% endcomment %} {% assign ing = site.data.cocktails.ingredients %} {% assign bottles = site.data.cocktails.bottles.bottles %}
